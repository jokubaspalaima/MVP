not2day is a self-help and support tool for people who are choosing not to drink or use, or who are working through an addiction. It offers a daily check-in, a moment-of-crisis flow, optional self-assessment questionnaires, and information about support meetings and helplines. It is a source of support and information only.

It is not a substitute for professional care.

The self-assessments (such as AUDIT, DAST-10, and the Gamblers Anonymous 20 Questions) are publicly available screening tools. They can suggest whether something may be worth attention, but they cannot diagnose you. Only a qualified professional can do that. Always seek the advice of a doctor, therapist, or other qualified provider with any questions about your health.
